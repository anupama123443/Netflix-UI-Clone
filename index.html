Frequently Asked Questions

What is Netflix?
Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime, documentaries and more on thousands of internet-connected devices. You can watch anytime and anywhere with an internet connection.
How much does Netflix cost?
Netflix has flexible plans to fit your budget. Plans start at ₹149/month for basic access and go up to ₹599/month for premium 4K Ultra HD. Watch on multiple devices with no ads. Cancel anytime.
Where can I watch?
Watch anywhere, anytime on your favorite devices - Smart TV, tablet, phone, laptop and gaming consoles. Use the Netflix app or visit netflix.com. You can stream on up to 4 screens simultaneously depending on your plan.
How do I cancel?
Netflix is flexible. There are no annoying contracts and no commitments. You can cancel your account anytime with just a click of a button. No cancellation fees – cancel online in seconds.
